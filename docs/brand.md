# VertexADE identity

VertexADE stands for **Vertex Agent Development Environment**. Its identity uses three connected vertices to form a direct, recognizable `V`.

## Mark construction

- The two upper vertices represent independent agents or worktrees.
- Their paths converge on one shared execution point.
- The resulting geometry is both a connected graph and the initial `V`.
- The mint output vertex distinguishes the result from the contributing paths.
